Statutes of Nova Tempora

An English overview of our association’s legal foundation. The full German text is the authoritative original.

Important: This English summary is provided for reference only and is not legally binding. The complete German statutes, adopted and signed on 20 November 2026, are authoritative.

Articles 1–3

Name, seat, purpose and activities

Nova Tempora is an association under Articles 60 et seq. of the Swiss Civil Code, based in Bern, Switzerland. It promotes knowledge exchange, networking and interdisciplinary dialogue about technology (including AI), history, culture and social developments. Activities may include talks, panel discussions, meetups, workshops, publications, projects, experiments and collaborations. Any economic activities are subordinate to the association’s purpose. The association does not operate for the financial benefit of its members.

Articles 4–6

Funds and membership

The association may receive membership fees, donations, sponsorship and income from activities and purpose-related services. Funds are used exclusively for the association’s purposes, with no profit distribution to members. Individuals and legal entities may apply for membership in text form; the board decides on admission. The statutes also regulate resignation and exclusion procedures.

Articles 7–10

General assembly

The general assembly is the association’s highest governing body and meets annually. It elects the board, approves accounts and the budget, and determines membership fees. Members generally have one vote each; the statutes set notice periods, attendance requirements, decision-making rules and special majorities.

Articles 11–13

Board and representation

The board consists of at least two members, each elected for one year with re-election possible. It may appoint two equal co-presidents. With a two-member board, decisions require the express agreement of both members. The association is legally bound by joint signature of two board members. Board duties are generally voluntary, subject to reimbursement of documented expenses and limited payment for separately agreed services.

Articles 14–15

Accounting, liability and dissolution

The financial year follows the calendar year. The association maintains accounts and is liable for its obligations with its own assets; members have no general duty to contribute beyond their fees. Dissolution and the allocation of any remaining assets are subject to the procedural and purpose-related safeguards stated in the German text.
