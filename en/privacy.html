How personal data is processed when you visit this website or contact Nova Tempora.

01 / Controller

For privacy enquiries, contact info@novatempora.ch.

02 / Website access

What data is processed when visiting?

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Technical connection data such as IP addresses, access times, requested pages and browser details may be processed when a website is accessed. Technical processing may take place outside Switzerland.

We have not added our own analytics or advertising trackers to the website source. You can consult GitHub’s privacy statement for information about its processing.

03 / Form.taxi

What happens to form submissions?

When you submit our contact form, we process your name, e-mail address, selected topic and message to handle your enquiry. The form service forwards the submission to our association mailbox.

For this service we use Form.taxi, operated by wrkt*biz Reinhard Söllradl (Austria). According to the provider, submissions are processed and stored in the EU, with hosting in Germany. The service may also process technical data such as IP address, browser identifier and submission timestamp for delivery and anti-spam purposes.

Form.taxi states that entries in its submission archive are automatically deleted no later than one year after submission, while entries classified as spam are deleted sooner. Correspondence received in our mailbox follows the retention principles below.

See Form.taxi’s privacy policy. Please do not send confidential or particularly sensitive personal data via the form. You may contact us directly by e-mail instead.

04 / Direct contact

E-mail and post

If you contact us by e-mail or post, we process the information you voluntarily provide solely to handle your enquiry and any follow-up communication. Our association mailbox uses Hostpoint Cloud Office, which may process technical and message data as part of this service.

05 / Retention

How long do we keep data?

We retain correspondence for as long as necessary to answer the enquiry, handle follow-up questions or fulfil applicable legal retention duties. We then delete data unless a legitimate reason for further retention applies. The form service also applies its own retention settings and policies.

06 / Membership

Membership applications and member data

When you apply for membership, we process your first and last names, email address, membership type and confirmations. For organisations, we also need the organisation’s name and the name of its representative. Your postal address, interests, ways you may wish to contribute and any additional comments are optional.

The board uses this information to review your application, decide on admission and let you know the outcome. Optional information helps us understand your interests and how you might like to get involved. Form.taxi forwards online applications to the association. Form.taxi’s privacy policy also applies to its processing.

After admission, we keep the details necessary for your membership in our internal register. We use them to administer membership, communicate with members and manage any fees. The register is private.

We do not add unsuccessful applicants to the membership register. We delete application data when it is no longer needed to handle the application or follow-up questions and there is no legal or other legitimate reason to retain it. The same principle applies after membership ends. To request access, correction or deletion, email info@novatempora.ch.

07 / Your rights

Access, correction and deletion

Write to info@novatempora.ch if you wish to request access to personal data about you, or ask for its correction or deletion. We assess requests under applicable Swiss data protection law.

08 / Updates

Changes to this notice

We update this notice when the website, the services we use or our handling of personal data changes.
