Nova Tempora · Founded 20 November 2026

Connecting people. Broadening perspectives.

Nova Tempora brings together people curious about the relationships between our history, today’s technologies and tomorrow’s society.

Who we are

Looking beyond disciplinary boundaries.

We are interested in the forces that have shaped our world – and those that will transform it. Instead of a single perspective, we value questions, connections and the willingness to learn from each other.

History, Technology and Society are three complementary ways of exploring these questions.

Our values

Curiosity. Openness. Dialogue.

Nova Tempora aims to be a place for diverse viewpoints and constructive conversations.

01 / CURIOSITY

Ask questions

Explore backgrounds, understand change and keep thinking.

02 / OPENNESS

Welcome perspectives

Different experiences and ideas enrich the bigger picture.

03 / DIALOGUE

Connect ideas

Respectful exchange makes new insights and joint initiatives possible.

Our purpose & activities

Share knowledge. Connect people. Develop ideas.

Our statutes promote open, interdisciplinary dialogue about technology – especially AI and emerging technologies – history, culture and social change.

01 / EXCHANGE

Create encounters

Talks, discussions, meetups and workshops for learning and critical reflection.

02 / PROJECTS

Build together

Working groups, publications, experiments and digital or technical projects.

03 / COLLABORATION

Build bridges

Collaborate with individuals, professionals, businesses, universities, cultural institutions and other organisations.

These activities reflect possibilities under our statutes; not all are confirmed events.

Community & membership

Become part of Nova Tempora.

People and organisations who support the association's purpose are welcome to apply for membership.

Under Article 5 of our statutes, applications must be made in text form. The board decides on admission; membership begins upon confirmation.

Membership fees are set by the general assembly. Please get in touch for further information.
