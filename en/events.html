  1. 20 November 2026
    First historic milestone

    Founding meeting – Where it all began

    FHNW campus · Olten, Switzerland

    Nova Tempora was founded on 20 November 2026 on the Olten campus of the University of Applied Sciences and Arts Northwestern Switzerland (FHNW). The association’s statutes were adopted and signed that day.

    This marked the beginning of a community that connects the past, present and future.
